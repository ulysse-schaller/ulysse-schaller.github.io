Ulysse Schaller

Hi! My name is Ulysse Schaller, and I do research in AI safety. I'm based in Zurich.

I'm a postdoc in the AI Safety Research Group at the University of Stuttgart. I've been focusing on empirical research; you can find a list of my projects below. My research is motivated by concern about risks from artificial intelligence, in particular existential risks. I also think it is plausible that current or future AIs deserve moral consideration.

Before moving into AI safety, I did a math PhD at ETH Zurich on spreading processes in random graph models of real-world networks.
